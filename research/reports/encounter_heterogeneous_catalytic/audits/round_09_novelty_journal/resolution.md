## Decision

Two independent reviewers searched and compared the closest encounter,
heterogeneous-reaction, gated-reaction, multimodal first-passage, capacity, and
Giuggioli/Luca precedents. Neither found a paper that anticipates the complete
combination claimed here. The defensible increment is deliberately narrow:

> With the transport generator and integrated killing budget held fixed, a
> time-independent spatial redistribution of finite-radius Doi reactivity is
> used to select encounter clocks and cross a derivative-certified
> reaction-time density fold; finite-grid 2D controls and a bounded
> three-channel certificate test the mechanism without claiming a converged
> continuum fold.

Green/Woodbury reduction, heterogeneous targets, fixed-total-reactivity design,
GIG hitting-time laws, capacity asymptotics, reaction-generated multimodality,
and multiple first-passage peaks are explicitly treated as prior art rather
than claimed contributions.

1. **Giuggioli/Luca lineage (B1, closed).** The introduction and novelty

   disorder-generated multiple peaks from resetting- and inert-heterogeneity
   reshaping of peak weights or mode location. The paper is positioned as a
   direct extension of Luca's encounter/heterogeneous-transmission program,
   with a new observable and matched reaction-field control rather than a new
   propagator theory.

   survival, rate, MFPT, and optimization observables are separated from the

3. **Reactivity-induced multimodality (B1, closed).** Grebenkov's
   encounter-history activation and Scher--Reuveni internal-state gating are
   acknowledged as distinct established mechanisms. The present control is
   static in time and spatially redistributed at fixed transport and budget.
4. **Channel terminology (B2, closed).** Godec--Metzler trajectory channels
   are separated from recognition-state channels, with the partial-reaction
   early/late-clock precedent cited independently.
5. **Foundational bibliography and metadata (B2/B3, closed).** Direct GIG,
   Doi--Smoluchowski, gated-FPT, partially reactive spherical-trap, and related
   sources were added. All 36 DOI strings resolved in Reviewer B's final
   check; the Grebenkov JCP article number is corrected to `244108`, and the

6. **Domestic journal metadata (B2, closed).** The journal note records the

7. **Submission checklist regression guard (B2, closed).** The PRE gate now
   requires all three prior-art contrasts: Giuggioli transport modes,
   Grebenkov/Scher reaction-law modes, and Nguyen--Grebenkov/Scott fixed-total

## Journal decision

**Physical Review E is the first target for the current evidence package.**
The headline is a statistical-physics/stochastic-process modality mechanism,
and PRE contains the closest literature while tolerating the honestly stated
finite-grid status. JCP becomes the stronger first choice only after a
grid-stabilized 2D fold, an independent Robin/radiation calculation, or a
sharply predictive chemical application. PRResearch remains a strategic
stretch requiring a genuinely stronger cross-dimensional result, not merely a
higher reported metric.
