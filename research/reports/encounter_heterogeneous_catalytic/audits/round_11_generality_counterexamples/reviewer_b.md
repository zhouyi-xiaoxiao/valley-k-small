# Round 11 reviewer B — literature priority and spectral boundary

Date: 2026-07-13

## Search target

I searched primary papers and official journal records for fixed-total
reactivity design, multimodal first-passage laws, phase-type density shape,
exponential-polynomial zero counts, recent Giuggioli work, and 2025--2026
Robin/Steklov/capacity results.  I compared them with the introduction,
novelty section, bibliography, and journal note.

## Findings

1. **B1 — missing closest fixed-budget precedent.** Nicolaou and Mulder,
   Scientific Reports 13, 22815 (2023), optimize a position-dependent Robin
   field under fixed total/mean reactivity to maximize steady flux.  The paper
   cannot claim first fixed-total-reactivity spatial optimization.  The safe
   distinction is steady single-particle flux versus a two-reactant full
   reaction-time-density fold.
2. **B1 — the spectral sign rule is classical.** For a finite reversible
   expansion `f=sum a_j exp(-lambda_j t)`, generalized Descartes gives a
   necessary derivative-zero bound.  It must be called a corollary or
   diagnostic, citing Jameson (2006), not a new theorem.  Bimodality needs at
   least three ordered residue sign changes and trimodality at least five.
3. **B1 — the spectral condition is not sufficient or nonreversible-general.**
   Complex spectra, Jordan terms, and infinite continuum sums are excluded.
   Existing phase-type shape work, including Yao (2002), must be acknowledged.
4. **B1 — spatial-disorder bimodality already exists.** Holehouse--Redner
   (2024) change hopping rates and exit routes; Marris--Hens--Ghosh--Giuggioli
   (2025 preprint) find a sparse-network bimodality regime.  The present
   distinction is fixed transport and redistributed Doi killing, not first
   spatially generated bimodality.
5. **B2 — current capacity/patch claims need the 2025--2026 frontier.** Recent
   Grebenkov, Grebenkov--Ward, and Grebenkov--Maurette work covers arbitrary
   partially reactive patches, Green matrices, Steklov problems, and reactive
   capacitance.  Capacity remains calibration, not novelty.

## Journal view

The spectral corollary plus a production-tested budget gradient materially
strengthens a PRE paper.  It does not remove the nonconverged 2D fold, missing
independent Robin solver, or chemical calibration needed for JCP, nor the
continuum cross-dimensional theorem needed for PRResearch.
