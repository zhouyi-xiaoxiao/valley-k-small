# Round 13 reviewer A — hostile PRE/JCP/PRResearch reassessment

Date: 2026-07-13

## Referee decision

The revised manuscript now has a coherent general finite-state layer rather
than only four selected spatial families.  The strongest headline is:

> spectral residues provide a necessary temporal-oscillation budget, while a
> fixed-budget modality gradient predicts a spatial fold-unfolding direction.

That is a material PRE improvement.  I found no new B0 correctness defect.

## Retained major limitations

1. **JCP/PRResearch blocker retained visibly:** M2D-F fold controls remain
   nonmonotone and budget-measure sensitive; no controlled PDE/SDE refinement
   or continuum fold exists.
2. Cell Péclet numbers remain large, supports are binary boundary-node masks,
   and M2D-T patches are underresolved.
3. M2D-E changes detector-resolved observability, not the number of strict
   local maxima; the paper now keeps these statements separate.
4. Evidence still spans M2D-C/E/F/T families rather than one factorial
   continuum family.
5. There is no independent Robin/Brownian solver, Doi--Robin match, or chemical
   dimensional mapping.
6. The main PDF is 27 pages with 12 figures and four tables.  This is viable as
   a PRE Regular Article but should be shortened or split before submission if
   the editor prefers a more concentrated presentation.

## Journal recommendation

- PRE Regular Article: recommend external review after author metadata and
  release provenance are complete.
- JCP: not yet; require converged physical discretization, independent
  reaction-model validation, and chemical prediction.
- PRResearch: not yet; require a model-applied joint-C3 continuum theorem,
  budget-projected cusp rank, and an independent 3D realization.
- PRL: not appropriate for this broad evidence package.
