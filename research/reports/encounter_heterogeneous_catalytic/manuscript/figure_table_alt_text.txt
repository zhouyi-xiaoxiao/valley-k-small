Supplemental Material
---------------------

Figure S1. Finite synchronous encounter family for four domain lengths. The
early near-catalyst peak stays at nearly fixed time while the dominant
far-boundary peak moves later and the intervening valley deepens as the domain
length increases; channel-resolved curves show which catalyst supplies each
peak.

Figure S2. Overlay of the representative reaction-time density under raw
synchronous dynamics, Poissonized synchronous dynamics, and an
independent-clock CTMC. All three retain a small early peak, an intervening
minimum, and a larger late peak, showing clock-robust separation without
claiming a calibrated continuum limit.

Figure S3. Four-panel M2D-E two-dimensional matched-control comparison. A map
shows the two catalyst patches and starts; reaction-time curves compare
patterned and per-lattice discrete-state-sum-matched homogeneous models; four
finer patterned grids are bimodal, the coarse patterned grid is a shoulder,
and all homogeneous controls are classifier-resolved unimodal while retaining
subthreshold strict secondary maxima. Matched homogeneous rates approach the
finite-radius contact-tube quadrature reference.

Figure S4. Four-panel catalytic-coordinate sensitivity comparison. Three
panels overlay patterned and matched-homogeneous reaction-time densities
constructed with the physical midpoint and diffusion-weighted encounter
coordinates on successively finer grids; the fourth quantifies how the
corresponding near, far, and union reaction masks differ.

Figure S5. Four-panel mechanism-control comparison in the bounded
two-dimensional model. The panels contrast separated boundary patches, a
single far patch, co-located channel labels, an interior two-patch family, and
uniform reactivity, distinguishing resolved pattern-selected modes from
shallow strict extrema and transport-associated clocks consistent with a
boundary-return interpretation.

Figure S6. Four-grid three-channel multi-clock certificate. Each reaction-time
density has three strict maxima separated by two minima; the classifier
resolves all three on the three finer grids and calls the coarsest a shoulder.
Root markers and channel-resolved fluxes identify the maxima with the near,
middle, and far catalysts, while tail diagnostics show no sampled downstream
mode.

Figure S7. Four-panel two-dimensional finite-radius capacity calibration on a
flat torus. Fixed-strength data approach the predicted logarithmic slope as
the grid is refined, and fixed intrinsic rate data approach the inverse
target-area reaction-limited law; these are mean-time calibrations rather than
patterned modality results.

Figure S8. Four-panel three-dimensional Doi calibration on a periodic cube.
The panels show the exact pair-to-relative-coordinate reduction, grid and
cell-average convergence, inverse-effective-radius scaling along a coupled
radius-grid path, and recovery of the fixed-rate inverse reactive-volume law.

Figure S9. Constructive GIG channel designs for dimensions one through four.
For two, three, and four prescribed clocks, isolated-height weights produce
the same number of resolved maxima; derivative-root plots show alternating
maxima and minima and make clear that this is a free-space screening
construction.

Table SI. Detailed evidence-boundary table separating exact identities,
finite-matrix certificates, screening approximations, finite-grid results, and
conjectured continuum statements, with the limitation attached to each
evidence level.

Table SII. Parameter registry for the four distinct bounded two-dimensional
family IDs M2D-E, M2D-F, M2D-C, and M2D-T, with separate rows for all five
M2D-C branches. It lists contact radius, starts, walker drifts, and catalyst
centres, radii, and intrinsic rates; the uniform row is the unit-domain member.

Table SIII. Five-grid M2D-E equal-budget endpoint audit. For each grid it lists
the patterned morphology call and two strict maximum times, the accepted
homogeneous peak, the number of multiscale views with two accepted peaks, and
the relative discrete budget error.

Table SIV. Positive-time detected sign-changing derivative roots for the
three-patch M2D-T family on four grids. Each row lists three maxima, dominated
in order by near, middle, and far channel flux, and the two intervening minima;
the finite search is not an interval-exhaustive root certificate.
