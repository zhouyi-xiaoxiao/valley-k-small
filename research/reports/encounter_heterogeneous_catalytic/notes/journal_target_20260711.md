# Target-journal decision — revised 2026-07-13

## Decision

**Primary target for the present evidence package: Physical Review E (PRE).**

The current headline is a stochastic-process/statistical-physics mechanism:
transport and a per-lattice discrete state-sum killing budget are held fixed,
static spatial Doi reactivity
is redistributed, and a reaction-time density crosses a derivative-certified
fold. PRE contains the closest first-passage and multimodality literature and
is the best fit for a finite-state bifurcation/mechanism paper. This is a fit
judgment, not a guarantee of acceptance: the current 2D lattices are not a
controlled continuum-SDE refinement and must be presented as such.

The July 13 upgrade adds a finite reversible spectral sign-variation
diagnostic, a fixed-budget Fréchet--Duhamel modality gradient with a closed-form
local optimizer, production-model spectral decompositions, and adversarial
counterexamples to sufficiency and channel-rank mode bounds. This makes the
paper more general and more predictive, but it does not change the primary
target: the strongest spatial folds remain finite-grid and nonconverged.

**Conditional higher-chemistry route: The Journal of Chemical Physics (JCP).**
JCP becomes the better first choice after a cell-averaged refinement campaign
stabilizes the 2D fold (preferably with an independent Robin/radiation solver)
or after a sharper chemical application makes the finite-grid mechanism a
predictive chemical-physics method.

**Stretch target: Physical Review Research (PRResearch).**  A higher impact
factor alone is not a reason to choose it.  It becomes credible only if the
final submission contains a genuinely cross-dimensional theorem, a continued
finite-radius 2D fold, the 3D capacity result, and unusually complete formal
and reproducibility evidence.  It is fully open access and requires an APC.

### Administrative lookup rule

Third-party pages, JCR quartiles, impact factors, and old repository labels are
not substitutes for an authenticated historical record or a written 2026+
institutional rule. Until those are captured, no numerical “中科院几区”
statement is treated as verified current metadata.

Scientific fit is therefore decided independently: PRE is the present first
choice; JCP is conditional on the stronger continuum/chemical layer; and
PRResearch is not recommended for the current package.

## Submission gate for the present PRE route

Submit the current package to PRE only when all of the following are true:

1. the novelty paragraph explicitly distinguishes (i) Giuggioli's
   transport-generated peaks and mode reshaping, (ii) Grebenkov's
   encounter-history activation and Scher--Reuveni internal-state gating, and
   (iii) Nguyen--Grebenkov/Scott and Nicolaou--Mulder fixed-total-reactivity
   spatial design from the
   present density fold under static spatial, fixed-transport, fixed-budget Doi
   redistribution;
2. in the M2D-E equal-per-lattice-state-sum-catalyst-budget audit, all five matched homogeneous controls
   are resolved-unimodal, four finer patterned models are resolved-bimodal,
   and the coarse patterned model is a shoulder while retaining a strict
   secondary maximum; the strict homogeneous secondary maxima are retained
   below the declared 3% threshold, the product-control-volume controls remain
   non-bimodal, and the 3% rule and finite 101-cutoff sweep are described as
   operational rather than experiment-derived or a continuous-interval
   theorem;
3. the finite \(L=31\) CTMC physical fold has nonzero third time derivative and parameter
   transversality, with held-out `1/2` and `3/2` scaling tests;
4. all three M2D-F physical folds pass explicit dimensionless-residual and
   nondegeneracy gates; the nonmonotone 0.262 state-count range, 0.384
   control-volume range, 12x8 out-of-domain root, and bounded 10x6 no-root
   search remain visible in the main text;
5. the M2D-E/M2D-F cell Péclet diagnostics and the underresolved M2D-T patch
   supports are persisted and block every continuum-refinement claim;
6. the 2D logarithmic and 3D Newtonian capacity tests retain their declared
   finite-grid and small-target error bounds;
7. the manuscript does not claim a Doi--Robin equivalence unless an
   independent Robin calculation is added;
8. all data, vector figures, manifests, tests, Lean reports, and the compiled
   manuscript pass the final adversarial audit.

9. the spectral sign-variation result is described as a classical necessary
   corollary, not a new or sufficient theorem; complex-spectrum, Jordan, and
   continuum cases remain excluded;
10. the budget-projected gradient is described as an infinitesimal
    interior-rate result, with nonnegativity, binary masks, moving supports,
    and finite-amplitude optimization left open;
11. continuum fold persistence requires joint `C^3(t, control)` convergence
    (or `C^1` convergence of `(f_t,f_tt)`), not merely `C^2` convergence of the
    density.

## Higher-journal gate after the generality upgrade

The revised manuscript is a stronger PRE Regular Article because it now has a
dimension-independent finite-state necessary condition and an inverse-design
calculus rather than only a collection of examples. It is still not a JCP or
PRResearch continuum theorem paper. The next-paper gate is:

1. a cell-centred FV/FEM Doi family with cell-averaged contact and catalyst
   supports, physical cost functional, patch diameters resolved by several
   cells, and local cell Péclet number below one;
2. odd/even refinement of the same branch with convergence of
   `(t_c, theta_c, f_ttt, f_ttheta)` and the projected modality gradient;
3. an independent Robin/radiation or Brownian calculation with Doi--Robin
   matching and a dimensional chemical example;
4. for PRResearch, a two-control budget-projected cusp-rank test and an
   independently realized 3D multi-mode configuration;
5. joint `C^3` semigroup/parameter convergence proving fold persistence.

Without these items, the new spectral and gradient layers improve the
explanation but do not cure the continuum evidence gap. PRL remains
inappropriate unless the program collapses to one sharp continuum theorem
with decisive 2D and 3D realizations.
