# Literature-priority and generality audit — 2026-07-13

## Defensible novelty sentence

No located paper combines two mobile reactants, a fixed transport generator,
finite-radius Doi killing, a fixed discrete killing budget, spatial
redistribution of that budget, and a derivative-certified fold of the full
reaction-time density.  Every broader component has precedent.  The safe
increment is therefore the finite-model combination of:

1. a fixed-transport/fixed-budget spatial density fold;
2. a budget-projected modality gradient that predicts a local unfolding
   direction; and
3. a classical spectral sign-variation corollary used as a necessary
   production-model diagnostic.

Neither the generalized Descartes theorem nor Fréchet/Duhamel sensitivity is
claimed as new mathematics.

The present work is a direct extension of that program.  Its distinct control
is `fixed transport + fixed reaction budget -> redistributed Doi killing ->
density fold`, not the first observation of FPT bimodality or a new Green
formalism.

## Classical spectral and phase-type boundaries

  <https://doi.org/10.1017/S0025557200179628>.

  <https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s2-20.3.446>.

The manuscript therefore calls the residue-sign result a **corollary** and a
**necessary diagnostic**, never a new or sufficient theorem.  Nonreversible
complex spectra and Jordan terms remain excluded.

## Journal ceiling after the upgrade

- **Current manuscript:** stronger PRE Regular Article.  The new calculus
  improves generality and prediction, but the 2D fold remains nonconverged.
- **JCP gate:** controlled cell-averaged Doi/FV or FEM refinement, resolved
  supports and cell Péclet number, converged fold derivatives, independent
  Robin/Brownian validation, and a chemical dimensional mapping.
- **PRResearch gate:** the JCP items plus a budget-projected cusp-rank result,
  joint (C^3) fold-persistence theorem applied to the physical model, and an
  independent 3D multi-mode realization.
- **PRL:** not supported by the current broad 24-page evidence package; it
  would require one concise continuum theorem with decisive 2D/3D
  realizations.
