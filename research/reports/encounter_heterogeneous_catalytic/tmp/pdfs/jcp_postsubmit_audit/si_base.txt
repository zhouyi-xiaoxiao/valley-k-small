              the full coordinate and reaction-model qualifications, finite-support Green and spectral audits,
              generalized-inverse-Gaussian screening construction, finite-state discovery and clock controls, com-
              plete two-dimensional discretization and endpoint families, capacity calibrations, multichannel
              screening calculations, normal-form derivations, provenance boundary, and limitations. The main
              article retains only the Green–Woodbury reduction, the necessary spectral gate, fixed-budget sensi-
              tivity, the finite CTMC fold, and the M2D-F finite-generator fold certificate. All results here remain
              finite-model or screening statements unless an exact identity is explicitly identified; no continuum
              modality theorem is asserted.
