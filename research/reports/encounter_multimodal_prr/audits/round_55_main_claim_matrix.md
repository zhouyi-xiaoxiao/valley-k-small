## 3. Evidence vocabulary used in this audit

- **PASS-ANALYTICAL:** a theorem is closed under its displayed hypotheses; this
  does not validate a practical finite parameter.
- **PASS-LEADING-ORDER:** an exact or converged `B\downarrow0` normalized
  free-exposure statement; it is not a positive-event-mass killed law.
- **PASS-DIAGNOSTIC-ONLY:** a correct finite-grid or implementation result that
  cannot support the focused physical claim.
- **CONDITIONAL-PASS:** wording may be used only after the named canonical audit
  returns PASS; no outcome is presumed here.
- **HOLD:** required scientific evidence is missing, not run, not independently
  audited, or blocked by open P0/P1 findings.
- **PRR-READY:** all same-family theory, finite-parameter control, convergence,
  independence, uncertainty, narrative, overlap, and release gates below pass.

The word `continuum` has two distinct uses and must not be collapsed:

1. the analytical theorem and exact free kernel are continuum objects; and
2. positive-`B` **numerical continuum/unbounded-domain validation** remains a
   separate empirical or numerical-analysis obligation.

## 5. What a canonical fixed-control PASS changes immediately—and what it does not

This is the most important rewrite boundary.

### 5.1 Claims unlocked immediately after the canonical independent auditor passes

Only the following may be added, using the released values and exact root/mass
window:

1. The broad `B\downarrow0`-designed geometry and allocation were held fixed at
   `B=0.01`.
2. On the two named held-out odd meshes in the same reflected box and SG/FV
   solver family, the released point passed the frozen five-root, relative
   prominence, curvature/residual, survival/conservation, and three-basin
   event-mass rules.
3. The two meshes passed the frozen agreement rules.
4. The strongest scientific summary is **at least three event-mass-qualified
   modes on the declared finite window for one fixed semidiscrete control**.

The main manuscript may then replace one open-gate sentence with a short,
ordinary Methods/Results paragraph. The precise reader-facing provenance should
be: the `B\downarrow0` calculation selected the design; the positive-`B` test
used unchanged inputs on two held-out meshes. Internal phrases such as
`PASS_INDEPENDENT_RECONSTRUCTION` belong in the reproducibility record, not the
published prose.

### 5.2 Claims that remain locked after that PASS

A fixed-control PASS does **not** unlock any of the following:

- `allocation cusp`, `cusp control`, or `cusp-organized finite-B transition`;
- either outgoing fold branch or a finite-`B` phase diagram;
- a claim that the remote pair persists across allocation controls;
- mesh convergence, parity/alignment robustness, box convergence, continuum
  extrapolation, or an unbounded-domain result;
- independent killed-process verification;
- physical-`d=3` finite-budget modality;
- globally exactly three modes; or
- `PRR-ready`, `resolved`, `robust`, or unqualified physical `controls` language.

Therefore a canonical fixed-control PASS does **not** authorize the current
title/abstract to be upgraded to a cusp-centered claim. It only makes a
theorem-first internal draft with one positive-`B` realization more complete.
