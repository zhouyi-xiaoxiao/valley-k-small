## 1. Paper-level claim ceiling

The focused paper has exactly two claim layers:

1. the independently accepted exact-`m` Doi theorem, at the hypotheses and
   sequential limits stated in the frozen reader-facing proof; and
2. at most finite-window, continuum-consistent numerical evidence for the
   physical-`d=2` model at the installed dyadic budget labelled `B=0.01`.

The numerical layer is restricted to the declared topology window
`[0.5,35]`; survival, basin-mass, and tail checks may extend to `t=100` but do
not exclude later extrema.  It does not claim a cusp, phase diagram,
finite-parameter arbitrary dimension, positive-budget physical `d=3`,
unbounded-time topology, or a rigorous numerical continuum limit.

Strict C0--C3 estimates and componentwise root transfer are activated only if
the paper elects to claim a rigorous finite-volume-to-unbounded-continuum
numerical topology theorem.  They are not a default release conjunct for this
focused claim ceiling.

The scientific campaign is the Cartesian product of the three canonical
exact-rational controls `lp_m1`, `lp_m2`, and `lp_m3` with the twelve

## 3. Minimum credible F0

F0 is science-free.  It must not evaluate any of the three positive-budget
controls on a production configuration.  It must establish, on synthetic or
control-free fixtures and on the actual largest-shape resource schedule:

- canonical packed inputs with exact type, ownership, layout, endian, length,
  digest, signed-zero, and nonfinite checks;
- a full rate-defined killed tensor operator whose diagonal is derived only
  from directed off-diagonal rates and killing;
- sub-Markov structure, detailed balance, mass and positivity semantics;
- bounded-memory directed `P^T` and `Q^T` actions with complete roundoff and
  rate-interval ledgers;
- absolute-time uniformization, directed Poisson-tail enclosure, and
  `J0`--`J3` plus `M2`--`M4` scalar jets without state chaining;
- complete-window tile exclusion, immutable root roles, curvature
  classification, twelve serialized interval-Newton steps, and zero
  unresolved time;
- two clean canonical replicas and an implementation-independent semantic
  replay from immutable inputs, with claim-field mutation tests; and
- a measured no-swap resource envelope for the actual
  `MR+F=(207,215,161)` schedule, including the complete topology call plan.

An implementation's own tests can produce `PASS_F0_IMPLEMENTATION`; only a
separate exact-byte independent audit can authorize construction of F1.
Hostile-writer cryptographic protocols are not a scientific requirement.
Ordinary immutable inputs, canonical hashes, fresh processes, bounded outputs,
and fail-closed local resource handling are required.

Each machine-readable branch carries exact required F0/F1/F2/F3 statuses and,
for a held stage, an exact `SCIENCE` or `METHOD_OR_RESOURCE` failure class.
No wrapper may choose a branch from prose, file existence, or a partially
completed downstream stage.

## 5. Completion definition

The submission wrapper must reuse, rather than edit, the Round-149-frozen
theorem sources.  It must consume append-only F0/F1/F2/F3 results or their
terminal failure receipts, select exactly one branch, and fail closed on
missing or mismatched hashes.

The manuscript is complete only when it has no internal gate vocabulary,
reserved module, placeholder, future-result prose, or unsupported figure.
The release package additionally requires:

- regenerated tables and figures from report-owned artifacts;
- a clean build and full-page visual PDF inspection;
- a rerunnable bounded reproduction path;
- an editor-facing overlap/priority map;
- final authorship, affiliation, funding, contribution, conflict, data/code,
  acknowledgment, and public-identifier metadata; and
- independent scientific, overlap, reproducibility, and rendered-PDF audits
  with no unresolved P0 or P1 finding.
