# Modal-certificate novelty addendum after the allocation-cusp HOLD

Date: 2026-07-13  
Status: literature-positioning note only; no scientific release authorization

## 1. Decision

The repaired modal-certificate route is a plausible organizing spine for a
PRR paper, but **the alternating-sign lemma and the linear program are not a
standalone novelty claim**.  The primary literature already makes four broad
facts unsafe to claim as new:

1. strategically arranged continuum traps can yield multimodal capture-time
   densities;
2. finite mixtures can have nontrivial modality and a mature critical-point
   geometry;
3. mixture-weight estimation with convex polyhedral shape constraints is an
   established optimization class; and
4. finite-state absorption-time (phase-type) laws are extremely flexible, so
   abstract existence of a multipeak Markov law is not physically decisive.

The defensible new intersection, subject to the prospective evidence passing,
is instead:

> fixed encounter transport, contact geometry, initial law, catalyst supports,
> and installed spatial-reactivity budget; allocation-only control of the
> complete finite-window modality; a full box-and-complement derivative
> certificate; a physical mixed-jet weak-budget transfer; and no-refit
> finite-budget plus independent process validation.

This is a search-based gap assessment, not proof that no overlapping paper
exists.  Any eventual priority sentence must use qualified language and be
refreshed immediately before submission.

## 3. What is general mathematics and what can be paper-specific

### Established or elementary layer

- endpoint derivative signs force non-strict ordered extrema;
- strict curvature plus endpoint signs isolate one simple extremum in a box;
- fixed-time derivative/height constraints are affine in nonnegative mixture
  weights;
- topology is locally constant away from multiple roots and time-boundary
  crossings; and
- fold/cusp normal forms and generic discriminants are standard.

These statements should be presented as transparent tools.  They make the
method auditable but cannot carry the significance claim.

### Potentially distinctive layer

The paper-specific contribution can be the full chain:

1. derive exposure clocks from a declared two-mobile-particle encounter
   process and a physical centre-space catalyst budget;
2. prove separated-clock feasibility for every fixed finite `d>=2` and fixed
   finite `m`, with the existing pointwise/sequential scope limits retained;
3. use an append-only, frozen selector to choose controls before their first
   positive-budget evaluation;
4. certify exactly one, two, and three maxima on one declared finite window by
   root boxes **and a signed derivative enclosure on the entire complement**;
5. transfer those inequalities analytically for sufficiently small positive
   budget using raw physical-unit mixed-jet margins and a registered monotone
   error bound;
6. realize the three topologies at one useful common positive budget with the
   same supports, transport, geometry, initial law, and budget, changing only
   allocation weights; and
7. pass mesh/parity/alignment/box challenges plus an unbounded off-lattice
   check of positive contrasts, survival, and event-basin probabilities.

The off-lattice method must not be said to prove absence of extra modes.  Exact
finite-window topology belongs to the deterministic interval certificate.

## 4. PRR claim ceiling and kill conditions

### Conditional PRR-facing claim

If all prospective gates pass without replacement controls or post-output
retuning, a defensible synthesis is:

> Conserved allocation among fixed spatial encounter channels controls the
> complete finite-window modality of the reaction-time law.  A constructive
> fixed-finite-dimension theorem and convex certificate identify designs,
> mixed-jet bounds transfer them into weak positive reaction, and same-budget
> finite-parameter calculations realize one, two, and three modes with
> independent process-level event-law validation.

### Claims still prohibited

- first multimodal first-passage or encounter density;
- first spatial configuration producing several FPT peaks;
- first heterogeneous reaction-time theory;
- first fixed-resource reactivity optimization;
- first convex or shape-constrained mixture-weight program;
- first discriminant, fold, or cusp description of density modes;
- exact topology from Monte Carlo or KDE; and
- arbitrary-`m` finite-budget physical control without a corresponding
  quantitative realization theorem.

### Route-kill conditions

The no-cusp PRR route fails if any of the following occurs:

1. a lower-modality control lacks a full complement exclusion, leaving only an
   at-least claim;
2. the three controls do not retain complete one/two/three stationary lists at
   one common budget under all frozen deterministic challenges;
3. the only positive-budget evidence depends on changing supports, transport,
   initial law, or the total installed resource;
4. the powered off-lattice event-law checks miss their frozen compatibility
   gates; or
5. a refreshed primary-literature audit locates the complete conserved-
   allocation/certificate/finite-budget chain elsewhere.

If the certificate layer survives but the physical chain fails, the correct
ceiling is a specialist methodology/design paper, not PRR.
