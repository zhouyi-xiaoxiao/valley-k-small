## 2. Executive result

The direct-overlap risk is **high**, but there is a clean PRR-scale route.
The current reduced GIG construction, Duhamel response, fold/cusp equations,
and finite-grid motivation cannot be packaged as a new paper by themselves:
all have close internal predecessors.  The non-overlapping paper is the one
that establishes the following intersection:

> With transport, domain, contact law, initial distribution, and fixed patch
> supports frozen, redistribute only a nonnegative static Doi encounter
> reactivity under one resolution-independent full centre-space integral;
> prove model-specific continuum response and projected-jet persistence;
> and obtain an independently converged physical-2D cusp/fold phase manifold
> and a controlled physical-3D modality transition.

Within the internal lineage inspected here, the arbitrary-finite-`m` GIG
theorem is a new analytical strengthening of an old screening construction,
but it is a design lemma unless a physical mixed-jet realization and
observability theorem is added.  This internal comparison does not replace a
final external mixture-literature search.

Luca's `Calculations` packet is **not a third independent scientific
predecessor**.  It is analytical source and correction history for the same
shortcut-to-absorbing-target lineage that culminates in DPMA.  It must be
credited as intellectual/provenance ancestry, but it cannot be counted as
independent validation or as a separate external comparator.

## 3. Classification used in this map

- **SHARED:** already present in a predecessor or standard background; may be
  stated compactly with citation/disclosure, but not sold again as novelty.
- **EXTENSION:** a real strengthening of a predecessor result, but too close
  to carry the PRR claim without the continuum package.
- **NEW-GATED:** plausibly non-overlapping only when its stated analytical and
  numerical gates are actually completed.
- **DISTINCT MODEL:** a new physical/numerical family, not a refinement of the
  predecessor family.  This prevents both duplicate packaging and false
  continuum-limit language.
- **PROHIBITED:** a title, abstract, figure, or claim that would duplicate or
  overstate the lineage.

## 8. The Luca calculation lineage

The source packet establishes the following facts and boundaries:

1. A shortcut from `u` into the absorbing target `v` is extra killing in the
   transient sector.  The corrected resolvent denominator is
   `1 + z beta(1-q) W_u(u,z)`, not the original minus sign.
2. The exact corrected symmetric generating function has denominator
   `a T_L(y)+U_{L-1}(y)`, and its dominant pole lies between the relevant even
   and odd no-shortcut eigenvalues.
3. The original proposed threshold `beta <= 2q/[(1-q)N]` is not an exact
   bimodality boundary.  Finite-time critical points, not only the dominant
   pole, decide bimodality.
4. DPMA subsequently supplies the corrected exact determinant/residue theory,
   geometry-resolved fold/cusp, and process-level validation.

Consequences for the new encounter paper:

- do not recycle the invalid threshold, pole-switch story, or `t alpha_1^t`
  tail;
- do not claim the observation that killing changes the full passage-time
  morphology;
- do not describe Luca's calculation as independent support for a continuum
  Doi cusp; and
- credit it as part of the internal analytical ancestry when the companion
  paper relationship is disclosed.

## 9. Claim and title collision map

### Claims that cannot be repeated as novelty

- first multimodal or bimodal first-passage/reaction-time law;
- first multimodal two-particle encounter law in 2D or 3D;
- first spatially heterogeneous or partially reactive encounter model;
- first fixed-total-reactivity optimization;
- first Green/Woodbury, Duhamel, rank-one killing, or spectral-residue
  analysis;
- first modality fold, first cusp, or new generic `1/2,3/2` exponents;
- first finite-grid two-patch fold or three-patch trimodality;
- first arbitrary-dimensional lattice encounter/search formalism; or
- arbitrary physical encounter modes inferred from the reduced GIG theorem.

### Safe working title before the continuum gates pass

> Working program for conserved-reactivity control of continuum
> encounter-time modality

### Safe strong title only after all PRR gates pass

> Conserved-reactivity control of cusp-organized encounter-time modality in
> two and three dimensions

Avoid a title led only by “multimodal encounter times,” “folds,” or “cusp”:
those collide directly with the external literature and the two companion
papers.

### Safe outcome-first abstract skeleton after completion

> Holding transport, geometry, contact radius, initial law, and catalyst
> supports fixed, we redistribute a nonnegative static Doi reactivity under a
> conserved centre-space integral.  In physical two dimensions this control
> produces a continuum-stable cusp whose fold branches organize the observed
> reaction-time modality regions; the projected mixed response jet remains
> quantitatively full rank under refinement and an independent solver.  A
> controlled three-dimensional transition obeys the same resource principle
> with dimension-correct normalization.  A constructive separated-channel
> theorem supplies reduced designs for any prescribed finite mode count, but
> arbitrary physical mode count is claimed only if the corresponding
> realization and observability bounds are proved.

The words “continuum-stable,” “cusp,” “full rank,” and “controlled
three-dimensional transition” must be removed unless their respective gates
have passed.

## 10. Minimum non-overlapping PRR package

The following are jointly required; none may be replaced by another reduced
scan or finite-grid example:

1. one frozen physical-2D three-patch family with a single full centre-space
   integral budget and no transport/geometry/initial-law retuning along the
   control simplex;
2. both fold branches and one interior, well-conditioned physical cusp,
   including the full fourth-order/mixed-control jet;
3. a persistent remote max--min pair and five alternating simple roots with
   event-mass, prominence, valley, and tail floors wherever trimodality is
   claimed;
4. a direct model-specific PDE differentiability/persistence theorem and a
   quantitative projected-rank lower bound;
5. odd/even or equivalent continuum refinement plus a physically distinct
   solver without parameter refitting;
6. a physical-3D fixed-budget fold or predeclared two-sided modality
   transition under dimension-correct budget normalization;
7. a final theorem/equation/figure/data/code overlap table, companion-paper
   citations or related-manuscript disclosure, and author-approved wording.

If items 2, 4, 5, or 6 fail, the project should be redirected to a focused
PRE/JCP-style continuum fixed-budget reaction-time-shape paper rather than
using the reduced GIG theorem or generic catastrophe language to preserve a
PRR claim.
