# PRR research contract

Date: 2026-07-14

## One-sentence target

This project pairs two deliberately separate claims: conserved-budget support
design constructs prescribed complete finite-window encounter-time topology in
an exact asymptotic Doi family, while a prospective finite-parameter physical-
2D campaign tests whether redistributing one fixed budget on one fixed support
selects distinct one-, two-, and three-mode laws.

## Distinct contribution relative to the preceding encounter paper

The preceding paper is the finite-state and finite-grid foundation.  It already
contains Green/Woodbury reduction, a spectral sign-variation necessary
condition, a fixed-budget Frechet/Duhamel response, finite folds, and reduced
GIG screening.  None of those ingredients alone is a new PRR headline here.

facts and gates:

1. an independently accepted constructive result producing exactly `m` stable

   certificate, followed by an independently powered off-lattice event-law
   check on the same common observables.

## Physical control variable

The control is a nonnegative, time-independent reactivity field

\[
  \kappa_{\boldsymbol w}(c)
  =B\sum_{j=1}^{J}w_j\phi_j(c),
  \qquad w_j\ge 0,\qquad \sum_j w_j=1,
\]

where each fixed smooth patch satisfies `integral phi_j(c) dc = 1`.  Thus
`integral kappa_w(c) dc = B` at every resolution.  Patch locations, widths,
transport, contact radius, and the initial law remain fixed while the simplex
weights are varied.  Grid-point count is not an admissible resource measure.

## Evidence vocabulary

- **PROVED:** complete proof under displayed assumptions.
- **NUMERICALLY VERIFIED:** deterministic calculation with stored parameters,
  root isolation, residuals, and conditioning.
- **DISCRETE DIAGNOSTIC:** a reproducible finite-matrix design result whose
  continuum and finite-budget claim flags remain false.
- **CONTINUUM-KERNEL CONFIRMATION:** a converged direct evaluation of exact
  free kernels, with floating-point root and quadrature checks but without an
  interval root certificate or a killed finite-budget PDE solve.
- **CONTINUUM-CONSISTENT FINITE-WINDOW EVIDENCE:** the declared odd/even,
  alignment, box, and independent-method gates pass on one hash-bound finite
  family; this remains numerical evidence, not a discretization-to-continuum
  theorem.
- **CONTINUUM VERIFIED:** gates C0--C7 of the current continuum research
  program pass under one hash-bound contract, including computable
  `r = 0, 1, 2` space, box, and evaluator errors and the strict root-margin
  transfer inequalities.  Refinement or independent-method agreement alone is
  insufficient.
- **IMPLEMENTATION SMOKE PASS:** software invariants and independent local
  references pass, without a continuum scientific claim.
- **CONJECTURAL:** theorem target with a missing quantitative estimate.
- **MATHEMATICAL NO-GO:** an explicit counterexample or regularity obstruction
  rules out the mathematical statement.
- **PROJECT GATE FAILED:** a predeclared empirical or numerical falsification
  gate failed; the corresponding headline is removed or redesigned.

No reduced mixture will be described as a continuum Doi result.  No finite-grid
fold will be called a continuum fold.  No three-channel cusp will be called a
trimodal phase unless five alternating simple critical points are isolated and
their prominence remains above the declared resolution floor.

## Sequential stop/go policy

1. Preserve the independently accepted exact-`m` fixed-finite-mode theorem
   with all epsilon-/`m`-dependent, compact-window, saturated-contact, and
   sequential-limit restrictions visible.
2. Keep the archived v1 failure and Round-118/120 repair chain visible; never
   infer complete topology from checkpoint signs alone or turn the existential
   `B0(epsilon)` into the numerical budget certificate.
3. Preserve all allocation-v6 files as a terminal negative result.  Do not
   rerun, retune, or use its failed cusp as a mandatory publication gate.
4. Finish only science-free F0 work before any new positive-`B` value is read:
   exact controls, common-observable selector, rate-defined tensor generator,
   contact-killing and full-operator assembly, fixed-memory propagation and
   jets, full-window topology, the production resource gate, schemas,

   already closed the production source-to-box stream, packed free-axis rate
   joins, declared-scope separate-source check, and two-repeat clean serialized
   replay.  Round 168 has frozen only the control-free contact/support factor
   bundle; its separate-source two-child acceptance remains open.  Those
   completed prerequisites do not by themselves constitute F0.
5. If F0 passes, execute exactly one sealed 36-row F1 campaign for the frozen
   same-budget one-/two-/three-mode controls.  A held row stops the chain; no
   replacement control, grid subset, threshold change, or historical-anchor
   rescue is allowed.
6. Build F2 only from an independently accepted F1 result, then require the
   powered two-pool off-lattice F3 event-law validation on the mechanically
   selected common cuts and windows.  The focused numerical paper is physical
   `d=2`; positive-`B` `d=3` is not required unless the headline says two and
   three dimensions.
7. Keep the global GIG-to-physical bridge optional: it is needed for a chosen
   GIG/universality claim, not for the proved physical fixed-finite-mode
   existence statement.

## Quantitative acceptance floors

- finest same-parity fold coordinates: below 1 percent relative change;
- odd/even extrapolated fold coordinates: below 1 percent disagreement;
- relevant third-order fold jets: below 5 percent disagreement;
- every stationary root: one interval-Newton inclusion, strict signed
  curvature, and complete complement exclusion on the declared window;
- each displayed secondary peak: at least 10 percent of the main peak;
- each valley: at most 85 percent of the smaller adjacent peak;
- mass-balance error: below `1e-9`;
- exponential-action residual: below `1e-10`;
- independent-process comparison: simultaneous common-window, basin, and
  survival intervals must lie inside the predeclared deterministic allowances;
  no continuous-density `L1` claim is inferred from trajectory samples.

These are project gates, not universal physical constants.  Any relaxed gate
must be justified before looking at the result, recorded here, and propagated
to the manuscript.
