APS requires disclosure when AI use goes beyond polishing, condensing, or
otherwise lightly editing text. Its policy asks the author to identify the tool
and version, describe the assistance, and explain how the author directed and
verified the retained output:

<https://journals.aps.org/authors/appropriate-use-ai-tools>
