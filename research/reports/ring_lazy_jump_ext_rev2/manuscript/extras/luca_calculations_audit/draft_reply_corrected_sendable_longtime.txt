D(y) = a T_L(y) + U_{L-1}(y),

F_rho(0,t) ~ B_{rho,1} s_1^{t-1},

where s_1 = 1-q+q y_1 is the dominant root-induced pole. Thus the beta-dependence
enters through a and hence through s_1; the corrected expression does not give a
generic t alpha_1^t long-time factor.
