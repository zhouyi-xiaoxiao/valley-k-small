For the symmetric antipodal case, I think the safest downstream correction is
to replace the long mixed alpha/gamma/s convolution expression by the closed
form whose denominator is a T_L(y) + U_{L-1}(y), followed by the corresponding
partial-fraction expansion. In that form the ultimate tail is controlled by the
dominant shortcut pole s_1, not by a generic t alpha_1^t term.

admissible range: the corrected dominant pole stays above gamma_1 and below
alpha_1. The double-peak range appears to be a finite-time shape issue rather
than a tail-decay crossing. Under a clear visual double-peak criterion, I see
